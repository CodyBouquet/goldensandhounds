Online booking

Booked online, straight onto our calendar

Online booking uses the same system we use at the front desk — so what you book online is exactly what we see in the salon.

  1. Pick a service & slot

    Choose your service and one of the daily slots. New here? The booking form will set up your pup's profile as you go.

  2. We confirm

    Your request lands directly on our schedule and we'll confirm it. Questions or special requests? Call or email any time.

  3. Bring your pup

    Arrive at your slot time and we'll take it from there. Booking ahead matters most around the holidays — those weeks fill up fast.
